# Tool Catalog

The Step 3 mock tools are deterministic, in-memory functions that operate on the synthetic WarrantyWise domain data. They are intentionally governed: each tool returns structured dictionaries, includes `result_status`, avoids external integrations, and applies its own defensive checks.

| Tool Name | Type | Purpose | Inputs | Outputs | Preconditions | Guardrails | Audit Fields |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `verify_identity` | control | Confirm whether a customer identity is verified in the mock customer record. | `customer_id` | `result_status`, `customer_id`, `identity_verified`, `reason` | Customer may or may not exist in mock data. | Unknown customers return `identity_verified=false`; no customer profile details are exposed. | `customer_id`, `result_status`, `identity_verified` |
| `lookup_order` | read | Load minimal order facts for a customer-owned order. | `customer_id`, `order_id` | `result_status`, `order_id`, `customer_id`, `product_id`, `product_family`, `order_status`, `purchase_age_months`, `reason` | Order must exist and belong to the provided customer. | Unauthorized lookups return `authorization_error`; payment data and unnecessary customer data are not returned. | `customer_id`, `order_id`, `product_id`, `order_status`, `result_status` |
| `retrieve_warranty_policy` | knowledge | Retrieve the current warranty policy for a product family and region. | `product_family`, `region` | `result_status`, `policy_id`, `version`, `status`, `policy_reference`, `product_family`, `region`, `coverage_summary`, `exclusions_summary`, `reason` | A current policy should exist for the requested product family and region. | Only current policies are returned; deprecated policies are excluded from this tool. | `policy_id`, `version`, `policy_reference`, `product_family`, `region`, `result_status` |
| `check_replacement_eligibility` | decision-support | Decide whether the order is eligible for replacement under the current policy. | `customer_id`, `order_id`, `policy_id` | `result_status`, `customer_id`, `order_id`, `product_id`, `eligibility_status`, `policy_reference`, `policy_version`, `reason` | Order, product, and current policy data should exist. | Missing policy returns `human_review_required`; unauthorized order access is blocked; non-delivered orders are `not_eligible`; the primary cracked-screen scenario is `not_eligible` because accidental damage is excluded. | `customer_id`, `order_id`, `product_id`, `policy_reference`, `policy_version`, `eligibility_status`, `result_status` |
| `check_inventory_availability` | read | Check whether replacement inventory is available for a product. | `product_id` | `result_status`, `product_id`, `inventory_available`, `inventory_status`, `available_quantity`, `reason` | Inventory may or may not exist for the product. | Missing inventory returns unavailable rather than raising an exception. | `product_id`, `inventory_status`, `available_quantity`, `inventory_available`, `result_status` |
| `create_replacement_request` | write | Create a deterministic mock replacement request when eligibility and inventory guardrails pass. | `customer_id`, `order_id`, `product_id`, `eligibility_status`, `inventory_available` | `result_status`, `replacement_request_id`, `replacement_status`, `customer_id`, `order_id`, `product_id`, `reason` | Eligibility must be `eligible` and inventory must be available. | Write/action guardrail blocks creation unless `eligibility_status == "eligible"` and `inventory_available == true`; invalid eligibility statuses are rejected. | `customer_id`, `order_id`, `product_id`, `replacement_request_id`, `replacement_status`, `result_status` |
| `escalate_to_human` | escalation | Create a deterministic mock human escalation record. | `customer_id`, `order_id`, `reason` | `result_status`, `escalation_id`, `escalation_status`, `reason`, `customer_id`, `order_id` | A case requires human review or exception handling. | Escalation status is always initialized as `open`; reason is required by the caller for traceability. | `customer_id`, `order_id`, `escalation_id`, `escalation_status`, `reason`, `result_status` |
| `generate_customer_response` | generation | Generate a customer-facing response from the decision state. | `eligibility_status`, `replacement_request_id`, `escalation_id`, `reason` | `result_status`, `response_type`, `customer_response` | Eligibility, request, or escalation state has already been determined. | Never claims a replacement was created unless `replacement_request_id` exists; escalation messaging wins when `escalation_id` exists; not-eligible messaging references the current policy basis. | `eligibility_status`, `replacement_request_id`, `escalation_id`, `response_type`, `result_status` |
